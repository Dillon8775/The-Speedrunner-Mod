Important Changes:
- The Speedrunner Mod now requires Fabric version 0.16.14 in order to run.

Boat Changes:
- Speedrunner, crimson, and warped boats can now all be crafted like normal boats, however, they will not be fireproof.
- To make these boats fireproof, you must add a speedrunner paddle in the normal crafting recipe for the boat to make it fireproof.
- This allows the player to still be able to easily craft boats, but if they want a fireproof one, they will need a speedrunner paddle.
- Created separate animated texture for all fireproof boats, so that the player can easily tell if it's fireproof or not.

Other Changes:
- Added a search field at the top of speedrunner mod options and feature screens to easily search for different options and features.
- Increased random chance that the player will get the mining fatigue effect.

Optimizations and bug fixes:
- Fixed bug where lava doesn't do the correct amount of damage.
- Fixed bug where player can cancel out previous fire damage by entering a fireproof boat.
- Fixed bug where sheep drop way too much wool.
- Fixed duplication glitch where the Blaze Spotter drops a lava bucket (duplicating buckets) no matter what. It no longer drops a lava bucket when using on a disabled mode.
- Fixed bug where title text renders twice on some scrollable screens.
