Version 1.12 (Fabric 1.21.11):
DRAGON'S AURA POTION:
- Added a new status effect, called "Dragons Aura", which gives the player +5 hearts, deals x2 more damage, added armor points and toughness, and increased block interaction distance.
- Any entity that has the Dragon's Aura effect takes 50% less damage from the ender dragon, and will also admit portal particles around themselves.
- Players are immune to dragon breath attacks with this effect, and also immune to fall damage.

- Players are also immune to ender pearl damage with this effect.
- Players will not receive blindness on doom mode when throwing an ender pearl with this effect.
- All players' armor will have a 45% chance to ignore all durability damage per hit with this effect applied.

- Enderman do not get angry at players if they look at them if they have this effect.

- To craft the Dragon's Aura potion, you will need water bottles and ender matter.
- When brewed, the potion's duration is 4 minutes. You can extend it to 8 minutes with redstone.
- You cannot increase this potion's amplifier.

- Dragon's Aura potions can also be found in end city chests and from trading with Retired Speedrunners.

- If the player dies with this effect, their death sound effect is changed to the ender dragon hurting.
- If the player falls into the void with this effect, they will be levitated up extremely fast to have a chance at surviving the void. This only happens once, and the player can only do it again if they died and re-applied the effect.
- You can also obtain the "Immortal" advancement by doing this.

- Breaking doom blocks with this effect will not spawn the bad mobs.

- Credit to MannyQUESO for creating the texture for the effect icon.

- Yes, there is an advancement for obtaining this potion, called "Dragon's Aura."

- This potion has been specifically designed for doom mode (it works on all modes), to make the end fight actually possible.

INVENTORY PRESERVER:
- A new item, called the "Inventory Preserver", which keeps your inventory upon death!
- It has 1 durability point, so 1 single use per item.
- They can only be found in bastion chests, ancient city chests, and very rarely in stronghold chests.
- You can combine it with ender matter to increase it's max durability all the way up to 3. It cannot be repaired, nor can you add unbreaking or mending to it.
- Good for doom mode!

WITHERED ENCHANTMENT:
- A new enchantment, called "Withered", which inflicts any entity you hit with the wither effect.
- For each level, the wither's effect is longer. At level 4, you can inflict wither 2, and level 5 inflicts wither 3.
- Using this enchantment on wither skeletons increases the chance of skulls dropping (minimum is 13% chance, can go all the way up to 65% with level 5).
- Looting no longer works to increase chance of wither skeleton skulls, you must use the "Withered" enchantment to increase chance.

- The withered enchantment can be found in nether fortress chests, and from the Retired Speedrunner.

- With this, the wither sword and wither bones have been removed from the mod and replaced with this.

SPEEDRUNNERS WORKBENCH CHANGES:
- You can now add a Golden Upgrade Smithing Template to the workbench to remove all durability damage. Doing this will result in double the cost.
- You can now transfer enchantments directly to books.
- The workbench is now crafted with 5 speedrunner planks, a speedrunner block, and 3 speedrunner ingots.
- Renamed "Golden Speedrunner Upgrade Smithing Template" to "Golden Upgrade Smithing Template", as the name was way too long, and rendered off the screen.
- You can now upgrade golden tools to golden speedrunner tools in the speedrunner's workbench.

DRAGON'S FIREBALL:
- Yup, you guessed it. The dragon's fireball, as a throwable item.
- When thrown, it does exactly what the ender dragon's fireball does. Except, it's effect last longer, and it creates an explosion upon impact.

- To craft it, you will need 8 fireballs and a dragon's aura potion. You will receive 8 dragon's fireballs when crafting it.

- Renamed Minecraft's "Fire Charge" to "Fireball".

EXPERIENCE FRAGMENT:
- A new item, which can be obtained from smelting experience ores (you can get the actual experience ore blocks by mining it with silk touch).
- With this item, you can craft Bottles of Enchanting.
- To craft Bottles of Enchanting, you will need 1 experience fragment and 1 glass bottle. You will then receive 2 bottles of enchanting.

GOLDEN SHIELD:
- Added the golden shield, with an extremely fast cooldown, but low durability.
