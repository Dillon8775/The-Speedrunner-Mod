# Version 1.12.5 (Fabric 26.2)

# Dragon's Sword Upgrade
- The Dragon's Sword has a brand-new, epic texture.
  - The new texture is heavily inspired from the Ice and Fire mod.
- The Dragon's Sword gives additional armor and armor toughness points when held in the mainhand or offhand.
- Killing the ender dragon with the dragon's sword now gives the player the dragon's aura effect for 90 minutes.
- The Dragon's Sword now has an increased attack speed (1, instead of the normal for a sword, 1.6).
  - The Dragon's Sword also has a very high enchantability value.
- The Dragon's Sword now works on doom mode! However, Goliath and the wither still have to be dead in order to use it.

# Dragon Upgrade Smithing Template
- Added new smithing upgrade template item, which is now used to craft the dragon's sword.
- The dragon's sword is no longer crafted with a speedrunner sword, ender matter and a dragon's pearl.
- Instead, you must obtain this smithing template, put it into a smithing table with a speedrunner sword and a dragon's pearl.
  - You can only do it in a smithing table. It will not work in the speedrunner's workbench.
- You can find the dragon smithing template very rarely in ancient cities, bastion treasure chests, end city chests, buried treasure chests, and stronghold chests.
  - They are most commonly found in end cities and ancient cities, but still rare.
  - The warden can also drop the template when killed.
- The dragon smithing template can not be duplicated by crafting. It can only be found naturally.

## Luck Effect
- The luck potion is now obtainable in survival mode.
- It can be found in various loot chests, including buried treasures, desert pyramids, end cities, jungle temples, shipwreck treasure chests, and in underwater ruins.
- Luck potions can also be brewed with water bottles and a lilypad. You can increase to Luck II with glowstone.
- The luck effect now increases chances of getting good loot from doom blocks.

## Withered Potion
- The withered potion is a new potion that can be brewed with a wither rose or wither skeleton skull.
- When the user has this effect, their hearts will appear as withered, but will not take wither damage. Instead, any target that they hit will be inflicted with withered.
- Additionally, the player will lose 2 hearts per level of the withered effect.
- You can extend the potion by using redstone, or increase the strength of it with glowstone.
- This withered effect does not effect the drop rate of wither skeleton skulls from wither skeletons, only the enchantment does.
- The withered enchantment remains the same, with the same effects.

## Enchantment Additions
- The dash, cooldown and withered enchantment can now all be obtained via the enchanting table.
  - Dash and cooldown are pretty common, but withered is more rare.
- Wind charges now support the cooldown enchantment.
- Targets inflicted with the withered effect via withered effect or enchantment will not be inflicted with wither for longer than 10 seconds.

## Loot Table and Recipe Changes
- Doom stone is craftable with 8 end stone blocks and a flesh block.
- iCarus Wings can be found in end city treasure chests. It is *extremely rare*, though.
- Knockback sticks can rarely be found in end city treasure chests.
- The blaze spotter is now crafted with an Eye of Inferno and lava bucket.
- The piglin awakener is now crafted with 8 gold ingots and any "piglin loved" item (excluding a gold ingot or raw gold in the center).
- Obsidian only requires 4 igneous rocks to craft (instead of 9).
- Gravel no longer always drops flint. Instead, flint has a higher chance of dropping.
- Shipwreck supply chests will always contain a saddle and 1-3 pufferfish.

## Fireball Changes
- Dragons Fireballs no longer create explosions when the ender dragon throws it.
- Fireballs can no longer have explosion powers of 11 or greater.
- Fireballs that have a higher explosion power now have a cooldown based on the thrown explosion power.

## Doom Mode Additions
- Zombies, zombie villagers, husks and drowned can now spawn with fireballs, and shoot them at players.
  - They spawn like this more commonly on doom mode.
  - Zombies that spawn will fireballs will only have half of their normal health and melee attack damage.
  - Zombies can shoot a fireball every 2 seconds on doom mode, but only every 3 seconds on easy/balanced mode.
- New advancement for shooting a zombie's fireball back and killing it, called "Spare Me".
- Three "Zombie Minions" will spawn when Goliath reaches half health, constantly targetting the player and throwing fireballs.
  - These minions will have 25 health points.
  - They can only be hurt by the player.
  - The minions will call other mobs to target the player.
  - Goliath will never target his own minions.
  - You can still kill Goliath with these minions alive, but they will certainly get in your way.
  - The minions can only throw a fireball every 4 seconds, instead of 2.
- Upon world creation, each player that joins will be greeted with a doom mode message.
- Each player gets 2 minutes of resistance and regeneration when joining a world.
- Upon world creation, the time is set to night, and has a 25-33% chance to storm for 10 minutes.
- Additionally, for every player that joins, a lightning bolt will be summoned 5 blocks in front of them.
- Attempting to sleep in a bed when the player hasn't freed the end will fail, send a message to the player, and spawn evoker fangs.
  - The player can still set their spawn, but they will have to endure the fangs and message in order to do it.
- The Wither can no longer target strictly Goliath or the Ender Dragon.
- Goliath is now immune to zombie villagers, zombified piglin, and wardens.

- "That Was Fast!" for obtaining a cooldown enchanted item/book.

- "The Black Plaque" for obtaining a withered potion.

## Speedrunner's Wasteland Changes
- It no longer will rain in the wasteland biome.
- The biome's temperature has been changed to 0.7.
- The biomes downfall has been changed to 0.4.

## Structure Spawn Rate Changes
- Mineshafts, igloos, ocean ruins, trail ruins and swamp huts now generate more commonly like other structures.
- The Speedrunner's Eye can now locate pillager outposts, mineshafts, igloos, trail ruins, and jungle temples.
- Fixed an issue pillager outposts hardly ever generate.

## Items and Attributes
- All speedrunner-related buffs are now attribute-based.
  - This includes piglin protection, sculk shrieker protection, doom block protection, lava movement efficiency, ender dragon damage (from dragon's sword), happy ghast fly speed, nautilus movement speed, and the new features introduced below.
- All Speedrunner and Golden Speedrunner Tools (swords, axes, pickxes, etc...) deal +10% additional damage to speedrunner-imperative mobs, which include enderman, blaze, piglin, piglin brutes, and Goliath.
- Speedrunner and Golden Speedrunner Helmets give increased oxygen and submerged mining speeds when underwater. They also allow the player to see further underwater.
- Speedrunner and Golden Speedrunner Chestplates give the player (or entity wearing) +20% protection against speedrunner imperative mobs.
- Speedrunner and Golden Speedrunner Leggings give increased sneaking speed, similar to swift sneak.
- Boot movement in water and lava have been slightly modified, according to the dash enchantment or if the boot item is speedrunner boots.
- Speedrunner boot shrieker protection now applies to all living entities, instead of just the player.

## Keybind Change
- Keybinds have been optimized, and now support multi-key settings, meaning you can set a keybind to be CTRL + H, or SHIFT + H.
- Removed the debug prefix when keybinds send messages, to be less obstructive.

## Option Changes

  - "Lava Boats"
  - "Fireproof Items"
  - "Custom Data Generation"
- Renamed "Custom Biomes and Custom Biome Features" to "Generate Speedrunner's Wasteland"
- Renamed "Stronghold Count" to "Total Strongholds".
- Renamed "Portal Rooms" to "Total Portal Rooms".
- Renamed "Libraries" to "Total Libraries".
- Moved "Generate Speedrunner Wood" to WorldGen options.
- New option "Common Plain Trees", which strictly controls if oak trees in plains biomes should generate more commonly.
- New option, "Warning Messages", which lets you toggle the warning messages that are sent to the player when using a "eye" item.
- Renamed "Higher Breath Time" option to "Increased Oxygen".
