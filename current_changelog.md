# Version 1.13.1

## Changes
- Reverted back to Minecraft's default credits.
    - Developer note, from Dillon: After re-reading through default Minecraft credits, I believe that the story it tells ultimately points to Jesus. Towards the end, it speaks of the universe showing infinite care and love for the player, which is exactly what God does. I have made this change because I think this story is much deeper and passionate than the one that I wrote. Finally, to the person that commented on this mod, stating that my credits were proselytizing, I apologize, but I do hope you see the story of God and his love through Minecraft's credits. Check the bottom of the changelog for a deeper visual of what I see. :)
- Tweaked splash texts.
- Added a "Resource Packs" button to the resources screen, which includes a resource pack template for the speedrunner mod, as well as a "dark mode" pack.
- Optimized the Resources screen & Other Mods screen.

## Bugs Fixed
- The end podium generates at the bottom of the end on doom mode.
- Workbench error texture is squished.
- Tons of flint and steels can generate in nether fortress chests.
- Ender matter almost always drops when killing enderman.
  - In addition to this bug fix, ender matter now has a 27% chance of dropping when killing enderman, with a 3% increase for each level of looting.
